Admission FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

We understand that choosing the right school is a big decision. Here is a list of frequently asked questions to provide clarity on our admission process and school policies.

The admission process begins with submitting the application form, along with the student’s birth certificate, academic records, and recent photographs. After document review, parents may be invited for a school visit or interaction. Once approved, the student is enrolled and the fee and class allocation details are shared.

Yes, we provide school transport services for students from nearby localities and residential areas. Our buses are supervised by trained staff, and route information, pickup times, and safety guidelines are shared with parents before the academic session begins.

The school operates from 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM on regular working days. Students also participate in supervised activities before and after class hours, depending on the academic and extracurricular schedule.

We maintain a balanced classroom environment with an average student-to-teacher ratio of around 20:1, allowing teachers to provide regular attention, mentoring, and academic support to each student.

Yes, students are encouraged to take part in extracurricular activities such as sports, music, arts, debating, science clubs, and cultural programs. These activities help build confidence, teamwork, creativity, and leadership skills.
